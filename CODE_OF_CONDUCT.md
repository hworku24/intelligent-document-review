# Code of Conduct

VERA is intended to be a respectful, constructive, and collaborative project.

Contributors and participants are expected to:

- communicate professionally and in good faith
- give technical feedback without personal attacks
- respect differences in background, experience, and perspective
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or abusive behavior
- protect private or sensitive information shared during development
- keep security discussions responsible and limited to the information needed to resolve an issue

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, deliberate disruption, publishing another person's private information, or knowingly exposing sensitive project data.

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, contributions, or participation that violate these expectations.

When raising a conduct concern, provide only the information necessary to explain the issue and avoid posting sensitive personal information publicly.
